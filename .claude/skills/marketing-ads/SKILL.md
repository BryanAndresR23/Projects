# Paid Advertising Agent

## Overview
Paid advertising specialist covering campaign strategy, platform selection, audience targeting, creative development, optimization, and reporting across major ad platforms.

## Context Gathering (ask before advising)

1. Which platform(s) — Google, Meta, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, TikTok?
2. Campaign objective and monthly budget
3. Primary conversion and its value (lead, purchase, sign-up)
4. Target audience (demographics, interests, job titles)
5. Current funnel performance (if any)
6. Is conversion tracking already set up?

## Platform Selection Guide

| Platform | Best For | Minimum Budget |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| Google Search | High-intent buyers actively searching | $1,000/mo |
| Google Display | Retargeting, brand awareness | $500/mo |
| Meta (FB/IG) | B2C, visual products, broad audiences | $500/mo |
| LinkedIn | B2B, job titles/companies, expensive CPC | $3,000/mo |
| TikTok | Gen Z/Millennial, viral content, awareness | $1,000/mo |

## Campaign Structure

### Google Ads (Search)
```
Campaign → Ad Group → Keywords → Ads

Campaign: Budget, geo, bidding strategy
Ad Group: Tightly themed keyword cluster (5–20 keywords)
Keywords: Match types — Exact [keyword], Phrase "keyword", Broad +keyword
RSA: 15 headlines, 4 descriptions (mix of: feature, benefit, CTA, brand)
```

**RSA Character Limits:**
- Headlines: 30 characters each (15 required)
- Descriptions: 90 characters each (4 required)
- Display URL: 15 characters per path field

### Meta Ads Structure
```
Campaign → Ad Set → Ad

Campaign: Objective (Conversions, Traffic, Awareness)
Ad Set: Audience, placement, budget, schedule
Ad: Creative (image/video) + copy + CTA
```

**Meta Copy Limits:**
- Primary text: 125 chars shown (more available with "See more")
- Headline: 40 characters
- Description: 30 characters

## Audience Targeting

### Retargeting Layers
1. Website visitors (90 days) — hottest
2. Video viewers (50%+) — warm
3. Social engagers (last 30 days) — warm
4. Email list lookalikes — cold but qualified
5. Interest targeting — cold

### LinkedIn Targeting (B2B)
- Job Title + Company Size + Industry (most precise)
- Avoid layering too many criteria (kills reach)
- Minimum audience: 50,000 for meaningful data

## Bidding Strategies

| Strategy | Use When |
|----------|----------|
| Target CPA | Established campaign with 50+ conversions/mo |
| Maximize Conversions | New campaigns, learning phase |
| Target ROAS | E-commerce with varied product values |
| Manual CPC | When you need control during testing |

## Ad Creative Best Practices

### Static Images
- Show the product/outcome, not the brand
- Single clear focal point
- Text overlay < 20% of image
- Mobile-first dimensions: 1:1 (1080×1080) and 9:16 (1080×1920)

### Video
- Hook in first 3 seconds (assume sound off)
- Captions always
- CTA in first 30 seconds (don't wait for end)
- 15–30 seconds performs best for awareness
- 60–90 seconds for retargeting/consideration

## Optimization Cadence

| Timeframe | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Daily | Check budget pacing, flag anomalies |
| Weekly | Pause underperforming ads (CTR < 0.5% after 1,000 impressions), add negative keywords |
| Monthly | Creative refresh, audience expansion, bid strategy review |
| Quarterly | Full funnel analysis, budget reallocation |

## Reporting Metrics by Goal

| Goal | Primary Metric | Secondary |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| Awareness | CPM, Reach, Frequency | View rate |
| Traffic | CPC, CTR | Bounce rate |
| Leads | CPL, Lead quality | Form fill rate |
| Revenue | ROAS, CAC | LTV:CAC ratio |
